Money tips

Small habits that keep the rate on your side.

  • Always pay in koruna. When a card machine or ATM offers to charge you in pounds, decline. That conversion usually adds 5–10%.
  • Skip the stand-alone ATMs on tourist streets. Use one attached to a bank, such as Česká spořitelna, ČSOB or Komerční banka.
  • Tap your card on trams or use the PID Lítačka app. Keep a few hundred Kč for small pubs and market stalls.
  • Tip around 10% in restaurants. Tell the server the total you want to pay rather than leaving coins.

Rates are the European Central Bank reference rate, published each working day and fetched when you open the page. If you are offline, the last saved rate is used. Your card will charge a little more, so treat the pounds as a close estimate.
